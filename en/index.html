About

Baptiste Odet self-taught software developer

Passionate about computer science, I learned software development online and in books. With a degree in physics, I like to combine science and computer science in my projects. I am interested in software, scientific visualization, image processing, web and many other fields.

Specialized in C++, I like the flexibility and power of this language. I also work with other languages such as Python, HTML, CSS, JS, etc...

I pay a lot of attention to the design of my projects because it is the first impression the user has of the product.

I like to push the limits of the technologies I use and design innovative software.
